About Kha-Boom!

We're revolutionizing education through interactive learning experiences

Our Story

The starting year of Kha-Boom! was 2025, and with reason made: to make getting a little math and science fun, interactive, and accessible to all. We believe the educational aspect is an exciting exploration- rather than dying by memorization.

Our team, forming from the Ho Chi Minh City University of Education, consists of educators, designers, and developers united in their passion for working with immersive learning environments and visualization techniques that expedite the grasping of difficult concepts through interaction. The combination of current educational research with advanced technology makes for a progressive approach to how learning is facilitated.

Our Approach

Studying at Kha-Boom! means they understand that the best learning happens through direct experience. Hence, all courses are designed to offer interactive experiences such as manipulating ideas, exploring concepts, testing ideas, and watching the consequences.

Created by a group of enthusiastic members from Ho Chi Minh City University of Education, the courses are flexible and adaptable as they try to meet students where they are and support them in learning at their own pace. This means it offers special help to you if you are a visual learner, someone who learns best through practice, or a problem-solving person.

By making education interesting and interactive, we help students build their confidence, ignite their curiosity, and develop an enduring interest in learning.
